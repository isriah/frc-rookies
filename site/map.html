Map

Most teams in the US, Canada and several other countries are placed at postcode level using FIRST's public team data; everyone else, and all events, are placed at the centre of their listed city. Items that share a point are fanned out slightly so each can be seen.

City locations from GeoNames (CC BY 4.0) and OpenStreetMap. This is not an official FIRST site and is not affiliated with FIRST.
